# From SCP to GA LAB

GA LAB is a product extracted from engineering work originally developed under **SCP (Self-Correcting Platform)**.

SCP explored a broader system: task execution, evidence, lineage, self-modeling, governance, learning, recovery and autonomous control. GA LAB intentionally does **not** publish that entire ambition as one product.

Instead, GA LAB applies a narrow subset of the strongest verification principles to one concrete question:

> **Can I trust this AI-generated code change enough to merge it?**

Inherited principles: Reality outranks model self-report; evidence is scoped and SHA-bound; a passing command proves only its declared scope; missing evidence stays `UNKNOWN`; test-harness weakening is itself a verification concern.

The private SCP/GA-LAB research repository and this standalone product repository have different scopes. GA LAB does not require a claim that SCP is complete.
